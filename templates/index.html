How a claim moves

  1. Photograph

    Upload up to six photos from different angles. The detector marks each damaged part and how sure it is.

  2. Review the estimate

    Prices come from the parts list for your car. Switch any line between repair and replace, drop a false mark, or add a part it missed.

  3. Claim

    Attach your RC book and policy. The policy number is read from the document, and an assessor approves or rejects with a note.
